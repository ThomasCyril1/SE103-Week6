Why smaller commits are better than one giant commit:
Smaller commits make it easier to understand the purpose of each change.
They help reviewers identify problems and understand the history of a project.
If a problem occurs, a small commit makes it easier to find the change that caused it.
Keeping each commit focused also makes debugging and future maintenance easier.
